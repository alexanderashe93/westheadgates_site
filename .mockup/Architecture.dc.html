The one way this goes wrong

Location pages that are the same text with the town swapped are “doorway pages”. Google demotes them, and it is the single most common failure of local SEO builds. Each town page needs something genuinely its own: a real job you did there, real photos, the actual travel time, local specifics. Build a town page only when you have a real job to put on it.

What actually moves the needle

For a local trade, your Google Business Profile and review count outrank on-page work. The site supports that; it doesn’t replace it. Claim it, fill it out, post photos to it weekly, and ask every happy customer for a review.

Why the guides matter

“How much do electric gates cost” is searched far more than “electric gates [town]”. Those readers aren’t ready to buy today — but they will be, and they will come back to whoever answered the question honestly.

The maintenance cost

28 pages of hand-copied nav and footer is not maintainable. This structure needs shared header/footer includes — still plain files on Plesk, still no build step, but one file to edit instead of 28.
