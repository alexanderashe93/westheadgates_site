Overview

What we’re building

A clear, fast three-page website that does one job: get someone who needs a gate to pick up the phone. No clutter, nothing to learn, and nothing that gets in the way.

Home

What you do, why people choose you, and a sample of recent work. Two ways to get in touch, visible from the moment the page loads.

Gallery

Your work, full width, filterable by type. Photos open full size. This is the page that sells the job.

Contact

An enquiry form that emails you directly, alongside your phone number, email, address and opening hours.

The thinking behind it

  • Built for your customer. Someone in their forties to seventies, often on a phone in their own driveway. Body text is set larger than usual and contrast is kept high.
  • Your phone number never scrolls away. On a mobile there is a permanent bar at the bottom of the screen: Call now and Get a quote.
  • Photographs do the selling. The layout is deliberately quiet so the work stands out rather than competing with the design.
  • Fast and simple. No page builder, no plugins to update, nothing that can break on its own. It will load quickly even on a poor signal.

The look

Colour and type

The scheme is called Ironstone — a near-black with a deep oxblood red. It was chosen over brighter options because it reads as established and serious rather than fashionable, and because it works as well printed on a van or a business card as it does on a screen.

Ironstone#1f1e1c
Oxblood#9c3327
Warm off-white#f6f4f1
Deep base#141312
Paper#ffffff

Oxblood is used sparingly — buttons, links and small marks only. Everything else is black, white and warm grey, so the accent still means “do this” when a visitor sees it.

Typefaces

Headings Bitter — a sturdy slab serif. Solid and traditional without being old-fashioned.
Body text Libre Franklin — chosen for plain readability at a larger size than most sites use.

Page one

Home

  • The headline says plainly what you make.
  • Two actions, side by side: request a quote, or call.
  • One quiet line of proof underneath — years in the trade, gates fitted, insured.
  • A large photograph of finished work, straight away.
  • Your phone number sits in the header on every page.
  • Four services, described in a sentence each. No jargon.

Page one, continued

Recent work and why people choose you

The four photographs update themselves. They are pulled straight from your gallery, so the newest work you upload appears here without anyone editing the page.

Four reasons, not twenty. No sub-contractors, a fixed price in writing, safety-tested and certified, and a workmanship guarantee. These are the things that decide whether someone rings you rather than the next firm.

Page two

Gallery

Adding photos takes no technical knowledge

There is no list to edit and nothing to log into. You copy photographs into a folder on the server and they appear on the website.

  • Drop a photo into the folder → it appears on the gallery, newest first.
  • Delete a photo → it disappears.
  • Put photos into sub-folders — Driveway gates, Railings — and those become the filter buttons automatically.
  • The file name becomes the caption, so oak-clad-electric-gates.jpg reads as “Oak clad electric gates”.

Page two, continued

Viewing a photograph

Visitors can move between photographs with the arrows, the keyboard, or by swiping on a phone. It closes with the cross or the escape key. Small thing, but it is how people expect a gallery to behave, and it keeps them looking at your work for longer.

Page three

Contact

  • Enquiries arrive by email at info@westhead-gates.co.uk.
  • Replying to that email goes straight back to the customer.
  • No monthly fee and no third-party form service.
  • Three layers of spam protection, so you are not chasing junk.
  • The form checks itself as it is filled in, which means fewer enquiries with a mistyped phone number.
  • Your phone, email, address and opening hours sit beside it.

On a phone

Most people will see this version

More than half of enquiries to a business like yours come from a mobile — usually from someone standing on their own driveway. The site was laid out for that first.

The bar along the bottom

Notice the two buttons fixed to the bottom of the screen — Call now and Get a quote. They never scroll away, and they sit where a thumb naturally rests. On a trade website this one detail typically brings in more calls than everything above it put together.

Scope

What’s included

PagesHome, gallery, contact, plus a privacy notice and a “page not found” page.
Self-updating galleryAdd and remove photographs by copying files into a folder. No login, no admin panel, nothing to learn.
Enquiry formEmails you directly, with spam protection. Works even if a visitor has JavaScript turned off.
Mobile and tabletEvery page tested at desktop, tablet and phone widths.
Search engine basicsPage titles and descriptions, a sitemap, structured business information, and social sharing previews.
SpeedNo page builder and no plugins. Fewer things to go wrong and nothing to keep updated.
HostingRuns on your existing Plesk server. No new subscriptions.
AccessibilityLarger body text, high contrast, keyboard navigation and proper labelling throughout.

Not included at this stage

Photography, copywriting beyond what is shown, online payments, a booking system, and individual pages for each service or town. Any of these can be added later — the site is built so that extra pages slot in without a rebuild.

Privacy noticeRequired by law once the enquiry form is live. We will draft it; you confirm it is accurate.

Worth being straight about

A website like this stands or falls on its photographs. Everything else is in place and tested. If the photographs are good, the site will do its job; if they are phone snaps taken in the rain, no amount of design will make up for it. It is worth setting aside an afternoon for this.

Next

How we get it live

  • You approve the design. Anything in this document can still change — colours, wording, the order of sections. It is far easier now than later.
  • You send the details and photographs from the previous page.
  • We fill everything in and send you a private link to review the real site before anyone else can see it.
  • You have a proper look on your own phone and computer, and tell us anything that needs changing.
  • We put it live on your domain, with a security certificate, and test the enquiry form end to end.

Afterwards

Adding photographs is yours to do whenever you like. If you would rather we handled it, or you want to add service or area pages later to bring in more search traffic, that can be quoted separately.
